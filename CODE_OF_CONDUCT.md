# Code of Conduct

Mainlagi Hub is a child-focused open-source project. Community spaces should be professional, constructive, and safe for people from different backgrounds and skill levels.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- be respectful and specific when reviewing work;
- criticize ideas/code, not people;
- provide evidence for technical claims where practical;
- disclose uncertainty instead of presenting guesses as facts;
- avoid sharing private or identifying data unnecessarily;
- respect licensing, attribution, and intellectual-property boundaries;
- prioritize child safety and privacy in project decisions;
- accept maintainers' responsibility to protect production, security, and product integrity.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized or exploitative content involving minors;
- doxxing or publication of private personal information;
- intentionally exposing credentials or security vulnerabilities before coordinated disclosure;
- uploading child photos, recordings, or personal data without a legitimate authorized reason;
- plagiarism or knowingly submitting material without rights to contribute it;
- repeated disruptive behavior after a maintainer request to stop.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreement is normal. Resolve it through reproducible evidence, tests, measurements, architecture constraints, and documented product goals.

A maintainer may reject a technically valid contribution if it conflicts with security, child privacy, licensing, product direction, or maintainability requirements.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments/issues/PRs and may restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported using `SECURITY.md`, not litigated publicly before a fix is available.
