# Trademark, Brand, and Character Policy

The open-source software license and the Mainlagi brand are separate.

## No trademark license

The `AGPL-3.0-only` license for Mainlagi Hub source code does **not** grant permission to use Mainlagi names, logos, service marks, product identity, or other branding in a way that suggests endorsement, sponsorship, or official origin.

This policy also applies to confusingly similar branding.

## Reserved Mainlagi identity

Unless separately licensed or explicitly released under another policy, the following are reserved brand/creative identity elements:

- the **Mainlagi** and **Mainlagi Hub** names as product/brand identifiers;
- Mainlagi logos and visual brand marks;
- official character identity, artwork, animation, voice identity, and commercial presentation for:
  - **Naya**;
  - **Gian**;
  - **Zia**;
  - **Paca**;
  - **Gavi**;
- premium curriculum branding and proprietary commercial content packs;
- official store/merchandising identity.

## What forks may do

Subject to the applicable source-code license, a fork may describe itself factually as derived from Mainlagi Hub.

A fork should use its own product name and branding and should not present itself as the official Mainlagi service/product unless written permission has been granted.

Examples of acceptable descriptive wording:

- “Forked from Mainlagi Hub.”
- “Based on the Mainlagi Hub open-source codebase.”

Examples that require permission:

- presenting a fork as “Official Mainlagi”;
- reusing the official logo as the fork's product logo;
- using Naya/Gian/Zia/Paca/Gavi as the fork's commercial mascots;
- selling merchandise using Mainlagi character identity.

## Screenshots and documentation

Reasonable nominative use of the Mainlagi name in technical documentation, reviews, compatibility notes, or discussion is allowed to the extent permitted by applicable law, provided it does not imply endorsement.

## Character assets in the repository

If character/brand assets are later added to this repository, their presence does not automatically mean those assets are licensed under the source-code AGPL. Asset directories/files must carry explicit licensing/provenance notices.

## Commercial permissions

Trademark, character, co-branding, OEM, merchandising, or promotional rights require separate written permission when they exceed ordinary nominative use.

See `COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md` for the separate commercial software licensing model.
