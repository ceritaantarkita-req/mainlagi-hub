# Expansion Batch 11 — Letters/Menulis Wave B

Wave B raises Letters/Menulis from 25 to 50 playable activities by adding 25 meaningfully distinct activities covering uppercase/lowercase G–M recognition, case matching, alphabet sequence, visual discrimination, and representative letter-formation practice.

Learning-integrity boundary: 22 new activities are assessed through measured tap-choice or matching evidence. Three representative traces (`G`, `I`, `M`) are practice-only with `completion_only_v1`, do not gate stage readiness, and must not be interpreted as validated letter-shape accuracy or writing mastery.

Expected repository/live state after production closure: 458 total activities, 445 assessed / 13 practice; Letters 50 total, 43 assessed / 7 practice; 28 stages, 105 lessons, 105 packs, and 108 skills.
